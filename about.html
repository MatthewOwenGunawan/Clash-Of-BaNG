About

Clash of BaNG

Clash of BaNG was founded in 2025 as a clan for competitive strategy games. It was created by players who wanted to build a platform where there was teamwork, smart battles, and great friendships among gamers. The clan was successful because more players joined, attracted by the organization and competitiveness of the clan. Today, Clash of BaNG continues to expand while remaining committed to its core ideas: strategy, unity, and fair play.

How the Community Started

Clash of BaNG began with a small group of competitive strategy players. They aimed to dominate with discipline and teamwork. However, they got frustrated with disorganized gameplay and a lack of cooperation. This led to the formation of a clan with a strict strategy and a smart plan.


Initially, the clan had a few organized battles, and this eventually led to a competitive community with a clear role, leadership, and a common objective for growth and excellence.
